# 📊 Retail Sales Data Analysis — EDA

## 📌 Project Overview

This project performs **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on retail sales data to identify sales trends, customer behaviour, and product performance.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze and clean the retail sales dataset
- Perform descriptive statistical analysis
- Analyze monthly and quarterly sales trends
- Study customer age groups and gender distribution
- Identify top 10 best-selling products
- Analyze revenue by product category
- Find relationships between numerical variables using a correlation heatmap
- Generate actionable business insights

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## 📊 Analysis & Visualizations

The project includes:

- Monthly & Quarterly Sales Trends
- Customer Age Group Analysis
- Gender Distribution
- Top 10 Best-Selling Products
- Revenue by Product Category
- Correlation Heatmap
- Additional Business Insight Visualization

## 💡 Key Insights

The analysis helps identify:

- Sales trends and high-performing periods
- Customer demographic patterns
- Best-performing products and categories
- Relationships between sales-related variables

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

Based on the findings, recommendations are provided for:

- Improving marketing during high-sales periods
- Targeting valuable customer segments
- Optimizing inventory for best-selling products
- Improving underperforming product categories
